About the app

This policy explains how Tilt Cube handles information. Madsun does not provide separate in-game account registration or operate an online game-save service. Android 1.4.0 integrates Google Play Games platform authentication, described below. Gameplay does not ask you to submit your name, email address, contacts, photographs or microphone recordings to Madsun. Bundled third-party services are described separately below.

Game progress, settings and backups

The app stores game progress, unlocked stages, best turn counts and language preferences locally. Some progress and settings also have a local Android Preferences backup. App and device language preferences help select the displayed language; the app does not perform its own GPS or IP lookup for this purpose.

The app does not upload these game saves to a Madsun server. Android backup, restoration or device transfer may include app data depending on the operating system, device and your settings. Local storage does not guarantee that data stays on one device.

Game progress and settings have no fixed expiry. They remain until cleared, replaced or removed through app or Android data management. Android app-data controls can clear local data; restoration from a backup may bring data back. Restarting a puzzle does not delete all saved progress or settings. Local deletion does not request deletion of information held by Google or remove every backup.

Advertising and Google services

Since Android 1.3.1, release builds enable Google AdMob interstitial advertising at stage-completion boundaries. The app updates User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent information and shows any required form before requesting ads. If consent information does not permit requests or an ad is unavailable, play continues without an ad. The app includes Google Mobile Ads and UMP libraries. Advertising requests use non-personalized, conservative child-content protections, a General content rating, disabled publisher personalization and disabled publisher first-party ID. These restrictions do not mean that ads or the included SDK process no data. The Android advertising ID (AD_ID) permission and the AdServices ad ID, attribution and topics permissions are removed from this build. Removing permissions or blocking advertising requests does not establish that all bundled components process no information.

Google describes its advertising SDK as potentially processing network-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device-related identifiers. Processing depends on the service, configuration, platform and settings. Google’s handling, recipients, retention and international processing are described in its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Privacy Policy. This policy does not claim that bundled SDKs collect no data.

The app’s privacy menu provides access to this policy and privacy-choice actions. The privacy menu can retry the consent flow and reopen privacy options when Google requires them. The app checks whether UMP permits advertising requests before initializing, loading or presenting an ad. A status message is not consent to personalized advertising.

Google Play Games authentication

Android 1.4.0 includes the Google Play Games Services SDK for automatic platform authentication using your Play Games profile. Authentication failure does not block gameplay. Automatic profile-creation prompts are suppressed. Madsun does not create a separate game account from this authentication.

Google manages the authentication and associated gamer identity. Its SDK disclosure describes gamer-profile information such as a gamertag and avatar, as well as automatic analytics and diagnostics processing. Google states that Play Games Services data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. See the Play Games Services data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Android 1.4.0 checks platform authentication status. Starting with Android 1.4.1, the game also reads the authenticated Google Play Games account’s achievement unlock state and sends the identifiers of earned achievements to Google. Earned achievements are calculated from local best results and unlocked cubes, including existing progress, and are retried after an offline or authentication failure. Google stores these achievements with the Play Games profile. The game does not retrieve or store a player ID, gamertag, avatar, email address or friends list.

The game does not upload the campaign save, individual turn counts, star totals or leaderboard scores. An earned achievement can reveal that its published stage or star milestone was reached. Local progress remains separate from platform authentication and achievements; neither provides cloud-save restoration. Deleting local progress does not revoke achievements already stored by Google.

You can manage your Play Games profile and delete Google-held game data through your Play Games profile or Google Account. Clearing local app data does not delete Google-held information. Google’s policies and account controls govern its retention and deletion.

Age groups and availability

Tilt Cube is intended for teenagers and adults in the 13–15, 16–17 and 18+ age groups. It is not designed for children under 13. Selecting these target groups does not automatically prevent installation by a person under 13. The app currently does not ask for a date of birth or provide an age screen. Local privacy and consent requirements may still apply to teenagers.

Madsun intends global distribution wherever Tilt Cube is made available through Google Play. Actual availability depends on the store and region.
