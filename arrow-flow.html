1. About this app

Arrow Mellow (formerly ARROW FLOW) is a puzzle game. There is no separate game-account registration or developer-operated cloud-save service. Gameplay does not ask you to submit your name, email address, contacts, photographs or microphone recordings to Madsun. This notice covers the app and its advertising integration; third-party SDK processing is described separately.

2. Local progress and settings

Progress, puzzle states, records, preferences and advertising cadence counters are stored locally. Madsun does not upload these saves to its own server. Android backup, restoration and device-transfer services may include app data according to the device and your settings. Local data has no fixed expiry and remains until replaced or cleared. Restarting a puzzle does not delete all progress. Android app-data controls can clear local data; backups may restore it.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Android releases with advertising enabled use Google AdMob for interstitial ads at stage-completion boundaries and optional rewarded ads for in-game help. Test configurations use Google's test advertising units. The app includes the Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs. It updates consent information at launch, displays a required consent form and checks whether advertising requests are permitted before initializing or requesting ads. Where required, Settings provides Ad privacy choices to revisit your choices. If ads are unavailable, play continues.

Google's SDK disclosure describes processing of IP-derived approximate location, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device-related identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on SDK configuration, region and choices. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy for its handling, recipients, retention and international processing. Android advertising-ID controls are available in device settings. A local save reset does not delete Google-held information. Madsun does not currently operate a separate remote analytics service for this app.

4. Contact and your information

For privacy questions or requests about information held by Madsun, contact tjsrb424@gmail.com. Sending email shares your address and message with the developer and email provider; please avoid unnecessary sensitive details. Inquiry information is used to respond and handle the request. Rights and any required retention depend on applicable law. Google-held data and choices are managed under Google's policies and controls.
